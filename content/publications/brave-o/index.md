abstract: >
  We propose BRAVE-O, a Multi-Agent Deep Q-Network (MA-DQN) framework for joint
  Physical Resource Block (PRB) blanking and Virtual Network Function (VNF)
  placement in O-RAN O-Cloud deployments. Our approach jointly optimizes energy
  consumption across Distributed Units (DUs) and Central Units (CUs) while
  maintaining Quality-of-Service constraints for admitted User Equipments.
  Evaluated on the SLICES-RI large-scale testbed with a full OpenAirInterface
  5G stack, BRAVE-O achieves significant energy savings compared to ILP-optimal
  and greedy baselines.
