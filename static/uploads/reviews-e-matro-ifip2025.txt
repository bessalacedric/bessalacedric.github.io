======= Review 1 =======

Strong aspects: Comments to the author: what are the strong aspects of the paper

The paper presents a well-structured solution to energy-efficient VNF placement in O-RAN using a hybrid MARL framework. The integration of decentralized dApps with a centralized xApp ensures both local adaptability and global coordination, making it scalable. The use of flow-based optimization formalization strengthens its theoretical foundation, while Deep Q-Learning's demonstrated adaptability in complex scenarios highlights its practical effectiveness. The experimental validation on various O-Cloud network sizes further enhances the paper’s credibility.
Weak aspects: Comments to the author: what are the weak aspects of the paper?

The paper could better address the computational complexity of MARL in large-scale deployments. While Deep Q-Learning performs well, a comparison with traditional optimization methods would provide a clearer performance benchmark. Additionally, more details on how the framework handles highly dynamic and heterogeneous environments would strengthen its real-world applicability.
Recommended changes: Recommended changes. Please indicate any changes that should be made to the paper if accepted.

To improve the paper, consider discussing MARL’s scalability in real-world deployments and comparing its performance with conventional optimization techniques. Expanding on how E-MATRO adapts to rapidly changing network conditions would enhance its practical relevance. Lastly, providing insights into future integration with edge AI and 6G in more detail would make the study’s long-term impact clearer.

======= Review 3 =======

Strong aspects: Comments to the author: what are the strong aspects of the paper

The paper tackles the critical issue of resource allocation and energy efficiency in O-RAN, which is highly relevant in the context of 5G and beyond 5G networks. The paper provides a clear and formal definition of the energy-efficient, slice-compliant VNF placement problem
Weak aspects: Comments to the author: what are the weak aspects of the paper?

    While the paper discusses scalability, the performance of classical Q-Learning and SARSA deteriorates in larger, more complex scenarios, raising concerns about the scalability of those specific algorithms within the framework
    The performance of reinforcement learning algorithms can be sensitive to parameter settings (e.g., learning rate, exploration rate), and the paper could benefit from a more detailed discussion of parameter tuning.
